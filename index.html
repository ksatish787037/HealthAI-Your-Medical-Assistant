Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the HealthAI platform and billing.

Is my medical data secure? +
Yes. We utilize AES-256 encryption and follow strict healthcare data privacy guidelines. Your uploaded reports are processed securely and are never used to train public AI models.
Can this replace my actual doctor? +
No. HealthAI is a preliminary guidance and educational tool. It is designed to assist you in understanding your health better, but it is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis or emergency services.
How accurate is the Report Simplifier? +
Our system uses a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architecture. This means the AI generates summaries strictly based on the text found in your uploaded document and verified medical databases, drastically reducing hallucinations.
